GENERATED by `python generate_metrics_doc.py` from the build's own column list, `metrics_dictionary.py` and `config.py`. Do not edit by hand: change the code or the dictionary and regenerate (the test fails when this file is stale or a headline has no entry). One page per tab; the per-project bases at the end.

## Conventions every column shares

- **Trajectory.** Every headline on Master and A1-A4 is re-computed over FIVE consecutive trailing 90-day blocks (Q0, Q-1 .. Q-4) by its own formula, each Data reference moved k quarters back (trajectory.py; Trajectory tab). A trend cell beside each headline shows arrow · QoQ · 4-quarter log-linear growth (annual) · quarters improved (direction-aware). T12M sums the four quarters of a window total and averages a rate. Years to benchmark at the trailing growth, n/a with the reason (Jake 2026-10-12).
- **Windows.** Q0 = the trailing 90 days ending yesterday (UTC); Q1/Q2/Q3 the 90 days before each; a monthly series' Q0 is its three latest complete months. A flow's window figure is its SUM; a stock's 'now' is its latest stored value within its age limit (credibility.now_max_age: prices 2 days, daily stocks 3, weekly / monthly their own staleness).
- **Annualisation.** A continuous flow is annualised over the days its series ACTUALLY covered (sum ÷ covered days × 365); a discrete flow with a declared cadence as mean event × events a year (Jake 2026-09-28, 2026-09-29). Never ×365/90 on a young series.
- **Coverage guard.** A ratio or difference of two flows is BLOCKED (the cell says so, with each side's covered days) when either side covers under 7 days of the window, and — for a difference or a net rate — when their covered days differ by more than a tenth of the window. A DECLARED ZERO (config issuance_declared_zero) counts as full coverage (Jake's review of 97b29ae, 2026-10-11, item 2).
- **Sources and tiers.** Each series is read from the first source that answers, in tier order: 1 free API, 2 contract / chain read, 3 rendered page or manual entry (dated, sourced). Two tiers writing one figure on one day keep the EARLIER tier, except total_supply for the projects in config.TOTAL_SUPPLY_ONCHAIN_FIRST, where the chain read wins (Jake 2026-10-11, decision A4). A change of measuring point blanks a series unless a handover is declared (config series_handover).
- **Circulating convention.** circulating = total − treasury − team/investor unvested − foundation − operating reserves − burned; STAKED TOKENS ARE CIRCULATING (out only for free float); protocol-held counts as non-circulating. The figure in use is the project's own figure, adjusted to Jake's convention (e.g. Hyperliquid Foundation + Grants out; NEAR total − lockups LOCKED), wherever one exists; our on-chain set is the cross-check, CoinGecko only where the project publishes nothing (Jake 2026-10-07; wording 2026-10-12).
- **Price.** Market cap, FDV and the supply ratios use the spot price (price_usd 'now'); fee ÷ issuance and implied buyback tokens use the Q0 average price; protocol staking yield converts each payment at its payment day's price (Jake 2026-10-09).
- **Statuses.** Buyback / burn status labels are DERIVED FROM THE DATA (Jake's review of 97b29ae, item 13): halted since / SILENT since / active (measured Q0) / none measured in Q0 / none (declared 0%) / (declared; no series measured).
